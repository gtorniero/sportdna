Privacy Policy

What we store, what we don't and who we share it with.

Last updated November 18, 2026

Who is responsible for your data

RACKET DNA is part of SPORT DNA, a project of Beyond the Work Portais de Conteúdo na Internet Ltda. (CNPJ 69.746.447/0001-00), São Paulo, Brazil. For any request about your data, email hi@sportdna.com.br.

Your answers stay in your browser

Quiz answers are processed in your own browser. We have no user accounts and no database of answers, and your answers are not sent with the payment. RACKET DNA keeps your last result, what you unlocked and your preferred units (feet and pounds or centimeters and kilograms) in your browser so you can come back later. To delete them, clear this site's data in your browser.

What we store, and why

  • Orders: order ID, product, amount, currency, country (US), payment provider, status, the checkout link and a one-way hash of your access code. We use them to confirm the payment and unlock your report.
  • Abuse prevention: a count of payment attempts tied to a one-way hash of your IP address, never the IP address itself. It is deleted within 24 hours.

What we don't store

Your answers, body measurements, age, pain history, results and report text are not stored on our servers. Card details are entered on Stripe's checkout and never pass through SPORT DNA.

Analytics and advertising (only if you accept)

When you first visit, a notice asks whether we may use cookies to measure visits and ads. If you decline or ignore it, Google Analytics, Google Ads and the Meta Pixel don't load, no measurement cookies are set and nothing is sent to them. The test works the same either way.

If you tap Accept, measurement runs in a separate frame that has no access to your answers. Google and Meta then receive: page visits (with the page address and only the domain of the site you came from); quiz start and completion and which step you opened, never the question or the answer; the purchase steps (offer shown, checkout opened, error type if it fails, order created and purchase confirmed, with product, price and an irreversible transaction ID); the site language; and campaign labels in the address (utm_*, gclid, fbclid, gbraid, wbraid).

Server-side purchase confirmation. If you accepted before paying, our server may tell Meta that the purchase was confirmed: an irreversible transaction ID, the amount and currency, the browser ad identifiers (_fbp and _fbc cookies) and, when email matching is on, your checkout email hashed with SHA-256. Hashing is pseudonymization, not anonymization. Queue data is deleted within seven days.

They never receive your answers, body measurements, age, pain, results, report content or your email in plain text. Google and Meta may link these events to your accounts under their own policies. Personalized ads and Google Signals are turned off on our side.

Cookies. Always on: sd_consent_v1 and btw_capi_choice_v1 (your choice, asked again after 180 days). Only if you accept: _ga, _ga_* and _gcl_* (Google), _fbp and _fbc (Meta, up to 90 days) and btw_meta_subject_sportdna (a random number that lets us cancel pending sends to Meta if you change your mind).

Changing your mind. The Measurement on/off button at the bottom of every page reopens the notice. Declining after accepting turns measurement off, deletes the measurement cookies the site can delete and cancels pending sends to Meta.

Payment and email

Stripe processes card payments and asks for your email in its checkout. We don't keep that email in our database; when we need it (for example, to process a refund), we look it up in Stripe.

Who we share data with

  • Stripe: to process payments.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the site and our payment server.
  • Google Fonts: serves the site's fonts.
  • Google and Meta: only if you accept measurement, as described above.

Like any website, these services may log the IP address of visitors. We don't sell personal information. If you accept advertising measurement, sending purchase and visit events to Meta and Google may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law; you can decline or withdraw at any time with the Measurement button, and we honor Global Privacy Control signals by treating them as a decline.

How long we keep it

Orders: as long as needed to prove the purchase, keep your access and meet legal and tax obligations. Payment-attempt counts: 24 hours. Meta purchase queue and hashed email: up to seven days. Your cookie choice: 180 days.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or ask what we share and with whom, by emailing hi@sportdna.com.br. We won't discriminate against you for exercising these rights. California residents have these rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to opt out of sharing, which you can do with the Measurement button.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

International processing

We are based in Brazil, and our providers may process data in the United States, Brazil and other countries.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the current version.
